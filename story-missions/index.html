How to prepare

This is a tiny story, not a school presentation. Keep it clear, personal and easy to tell.

  1. Set a 20-25 minute timer. Stop when the timer ends.
  2. Keep the story short and focused. Share the essentials and one or two interesting facts.
  3. Split the story by the roles shown on your mission card.
  4. Practise once together. Each person must speak her own part.
  5. Do not read a full text from your phone. Look up and tell the story.
  6. If you use advanced vocabulary, send a few translations to the group chat in advance. Keep the list short.
  7. Speak only English. You may silently check a translation in the chat, but do not explain it in Russian.
  8. Finish in five minutes and be ready for one group question.
