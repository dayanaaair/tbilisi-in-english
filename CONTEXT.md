# English Trip to Tbilisi

Shared language for the first English-practice trip to Georgia and the website that presents it to Diana's current students.

## Language

**Trip**:
A small-group English-practice journey to Tbilisi from 5 to 9 November 2026, hosted by Diana with logistical help from her assistant.
_Avoid_: Camp, course, package tour

**Participant**:
A current adult student of Diana with English at B1 level or above. The first trip is limited to fewer than six participants.
_Avoid_: Customer, tourist, attendee

**English immersion**:
Using English as part of daily travel, shared evenings, guided experiences, and challenges so participants become less afraid to speak.
_Avoid_: Intensive course, fluency guarantee

**Preliminary program**:
The current outline of planned experiences; details may change while bookings and arrangements are finalized.
_Avoid_: Fixed itinerary, guaranteed schedule

**Budget guide**:
An estimated total of up to 50,000 rubles per participant for airfare, shared accommodation, and the main program. Food and personal spending are separate; the actual amount varies by departure city and final bookings.
_Avoid_: Fixed price, package price

**Trip host**:
Diana, the author and teacher leading the English experience. Diana and her assistant coordinate the trip, while a personal English-speaking guide leads the relevant excursion.
_Avoid_: Tour operator, travel agency

**Information site**:
A no-pressure, single-page introduction for invited students. It explains the experience without collecting applications or presenting a purchase action.
_Avoid_: Sales funnel, booking site
